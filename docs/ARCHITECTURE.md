# WITLOCK architecture

`contracts/Witlock.py` is the only deployable source and its class is `Witlock(gl.Contract)`. It owns the complete agreement state machine, issuer attestations, evidence bindings, consensus decisions, appeals, audit history, and settlement ledger.

Creation freezes the requester, provider, 1–8 weighted criteria, external issuer wallets, document digest, and ordered deadlines. The provider accepts. The nominated issuer publishes an immutable attestation inside the same contract; a party binds that attestation to the agreement, and the issuer separately authenticates the observed digest. This proves wallet publication and exact provenance fields while leaving factual truth to the bounded GenLayer judgment.

Delivery acknowledgement or a valid dispute moves an agreement into review. Sealing requires every criterion to have authenticated evidence. `gl.nondet.exec_prompt` interprets the admitted dossier and `gl.eq_principle.prompt_comparative` requires validators to agree on the complete `PASS` / `FAIL` / `INCONCLUSIVE` mapping. The contract calculates the score, result, and integer allocation; the model cannot choose money or recipients. Decision versions and every transition are append-only.

Funding and payout entry points exist only behind `custody_enabled()`. The current implementation returns `False`, rejects non-zero funding and bonds, rejects payment attempts, and leaves zero-value decision records reconciled without claiming a transfer. This gate stays closed until exact StudioNet transfer, finality, recipient, failure, ambiguity, and recovery evidence exists.
